Uma Engine & Senki

GAM250 · SIT/DigiPen

A custom game engine built in C++ and OpenGL with a cross-disciplinary team of programmers, an artist and a UI/UX designer, then used to produce a game, Senki. As product manager, I planned milestones, coordinated work across disciplines and kept the team aligned on scope and deadlines. I also designed and implemented the engine's scene manager, with an ImGui-based editor for creating, loading and switching between scenes. After an end-of-project playtest, we used player feedback to simplify the controls and adjust level pacing.

  • C++
  • OpenGL
  • ImGui

Kinect Museum Exhibit

Final Year Project · Nanyang Polytechnic/Singapore Philatelic Museum

An interactive exhibit prototype for a museum installation, built in Unity (C#), where visitors control flight by flapping their arms. I integrated Microsoft Kinect skeletal tracking with Unity to turn real-world gestures into in-game controls. Working with a small team, I tested, debugged and refined the gameplay and gesture detection so the experience felt intuitive and accessible for the primary audience of children.

  • C#
  • Kinect
